About Us

Our Story & Dance Background

iDance Academy of Performing Arts was founded with a simple mission: to share the beauty, discipline, and joy of Bollywood, Indian classical and folk dance with the local community. What began as a small passion project has grown into a welcoming home for dancers of every age and skill level.

Our founder, Ishita Bhatnagar, trained for over a decade in Kathak and Bollywood dance under renowned gurus, performing on stages in India and across the USA before bringing that expertise home to build a studio rooted in tradition, technique, and creative expression.

Today, our instructors continue that legacy — nurturing confidence, culture, and community through dance, one student at a time.
